# Shooting guide (share with the user / their videomaker)

- Export the RAW clips: no captions, no stickers, no pasted examples. Send examples (screen recordings, B-roll) as separate files.
- One idea per clip, and change framing or location between sections: it gives the cuts rhythm for free.
- Start each take with 1 s of silence looking at the camera and smiling (useful for the cover).
- Say the structure out loud ("Vuoi più X? …"): the edit turns spoken structure into graphics.
- Leave headroom: eyes at about 1/3 from the top, so cards on top do not cover the face.
- Lavalier or phone close to the mouth; same mic for every clip (audio is levelled anyway, but noise is not removed).
- Screen recordings: iPhone screen recording with touches visible, slow movements, one action at a time.
- iPhone HDR is fine (it is tonemapped), but do not mix HDR and SDR clips of the same scene.
