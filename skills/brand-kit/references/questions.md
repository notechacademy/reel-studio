# Brand interview – question bank

Ask in 2–3 rounds. Skip anything already known (uploaded files, website, Project docs, memory).

## Round 1 – identity
| Question | Why |
|---|---|
| Nome del brand e handle social | label on covers and end cards |
| Lingua dei contenuti | captions, copy, transcription language |
| Logo (PNG/SVG) e/o sito web | extract the real palette instead of inventing one |
| Colori che usate già (hex se li sapete) | keep continuity with existing assets |
| Font che usate già | same; otherwise propose a free Google pair |

## Round 2 – content & voice
| Question | Why |
|---|---|
| A chi parlate (target) e cosa vendete | examples in explainer scenes must look like THEIR niche |
| 2–4 pilastri di contenuto | keyword per pillar for the CTA |
| "Io" o "noi"? "Tu" o "lei"? | on-screen copy and captions |
| 3 frasi "parliamo così" + 3 "mai così" | voice.do / voice.dont – keep their exact words |
| Meccanica CTA: commenta parola → DM / link in bio / DM diretto | end card and caption |
| 2–3 account di riferimento (solo stile) | taste calibration – never copy their assets |

## Round 3 – only if missing
- Mood in 3 adjectives (when no colours/logo exist) → propose 2 palettes, let them pick.
- Light or dark dominant? (default: light background 60 %, dark slides 30 %).
